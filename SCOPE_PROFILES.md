# Scope Profiles

Milestone 2 preloads immutable DNT TheOne 7–35×56 and Arken EP-8 1–8×28 families with MIL and MOA/BDC variants. Selecting a variant creates a separate user-owned profile with `REQUIRES_USER_VERIFICATION`; editing a verification-critical field changes a verified profile to `MODIFIED_AFTER_VERIFICATION`.

The physical checklist confirms model, turret unit, click value, reticle, focal plane, dial directions, zero stop, sight height and zero distance. Templates are starting metadata, not authoritative physical claims.

The Arken KLBOX remains `BDC`. It cannot produce a generic MIL/MOA hold and a KLBOX mark is unavailable until a cartridge-, velocity-, zero- and reference-environment-specific BDC calibration is explicitly verified. Dial output remains MOA at nominal 0.25 MOA clicks only after physical verification.
