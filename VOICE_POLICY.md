# Voice Policy

Deferred to Milestone 9. Android TTS must produce deterministic, structured, allowlisted messages. Clicks, MIL/MOA impact corrections, holds, aim points, lead, target selection and fire commands are blocked. Bluetooth disconnect must respect the speaker-fallback preference.

## Requested pistol-drill extension

A future pistol-training mode may play a user-authored sequence from a separate fixed cue vocabulary, for example movement, position and reload prompts. It may include an audible/random timer start but not a spoken command to fire, select a target, choose an aim point, or apply an impact-derived correction. Cues are stored locally, previewable before the drill, interruptible at all times, and never generated by a model.
